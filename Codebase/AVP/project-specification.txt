Develop a community project prioritization system.

The system manages different community projects such as road repairs,
health programs, disaster-relief projects, livelihood programs, and
educational support.

Project managers must be able to change how projects are prioritized
depending on the situation.

Projects may be prioritized based on urgency, available budget,
number of affected residents, or disaster risk.

The prioritization method should be interchangeable without changing
the main project-management logic.

Project managers choose different prioritization rules at runtime. Each prioritization algorithm is interchangeable behind a common interface, and the caller selects the policy without changing the project-management workflow.
